The 10-kilometer (6.2-mile) caldera path from Fira through Firostefani and Imerovigli to Oia is widely regarded as one of the most stunning cliffside walks in Europe.

However, hundreds of travelers set out every summer expecting a gentle stroll between villages, only to end up dehydrated, blistered, or needing assistance. 

Here is what you actually need to know before tackling the trail.

---

## 1. It Is a Trail, Not a Paved Sidewalk

While the sections through Fira, Firostefani, and Imerovigli consist of paved cobblestones and smooth stone pathways, the long stretch between the Prophet Elias church and the entrance of Oia crosses raw volcanic scree, loose pumice stone, and steep dust tracks with precipitous cliff drops.

* **Do NOT wear:** Slip-on sandals, espadrilles, or lifestyle slides.
* **Do wear:** Trail runners, sturdy athletic sneakers, or hiking shoes with deep rubber traction.

---

## 2. Zero Shade for Over 2 Hours

Once you exit Imerovigli, there is virtually zero canopy, trees, or shade along the trail. The Mediterranean sun radiates directly overhead and reflects off the whitewashed walls and dark volcanic soil.

* **Departure Time:** Start no later than **7:00 AM** in peak summer (July/August), or leave around **4:30 PM** to catch golden hour as you arrive into Oia. Avoid walking between 11:00 AM and 3:00 PM at all costs.

---

## 3. Water Stops Are Extremely Limited

Between the snack cantina at the edge of Imerovigli and the first small market on the outskirts of Oia, there are zero replenishment points. 
* Carry at least **1.5 liters of water per person** (or an electrolyte hydration pack).
* Pack high-SPF sunscreen and a wide-brim hat that won't blow off in Aegean gusts.

---

## 4. The Elevation Gain Is Deceptive

The hike descends toward Oia overall, but it involves several steep climbs, particularly ascending the volcanic saddle near the Chapel of Panagia Mavrokatavatis and Skaros Rock. Total moving time usually runs between **2.5 to 4 hours**, depending on how often you pause for photos.

---

## 5. Returning to Your Base

Once you arrive in Oia, remember that thousands of other hikers and sunset watchers will also need to return to Fira. The local KTEL bus from Oia back to Fira runs regularly, but lines after sunset can stretch around the bus terminal. If you don't want to wait, plan dinner in Oia and catch an off-peak bus later in the evening.
