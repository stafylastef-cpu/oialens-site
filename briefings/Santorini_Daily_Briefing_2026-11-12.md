## 1. Executive Summary: What Travelers are Asking & Complaining About (Last 24h)

From recent discussions across `r/Santorini`, `r/GreeceTravel`, and Mediterranean travel forums, key traveler pain points and trending concerns center around:

1. **Cruise Ship Influx & Photo Line Delays:** Multiple travelers complaining that arriving in Oia between 11:00 AM and 4:00 PM felt like "Disneyland in peak season," with 30-to-45-minute lines just to take a 10-second photo in front of the primary Blue Domes.
2. **Where to Base Yourself (Oia vs. Fira vs. Imerovigli):** A continuous debate among first-timers trying to balance caldera views vs. bus transit accessibility.
3. **The Fira Donkey Controversy & Port Stairs:** Intense discussions regarding the 588 steep steps from the Old Port to Fira, animal welfare concerns with the working donkeys, and long queues at the Fira Cable Car.
4. **"Is 3 Days Too Long?":** Travelers struggling to budget their island hopping schedules, asking how many days Santorini actually deserves before diminishing returns set in.
5. **Volcanic Beach Reality Check:** Disappointment from travelers expecting soft Caribbean-style white sand, only to find black basalt gravel, scorching hot pebbles, and cliff rockfall warning zones at Red Beach.
